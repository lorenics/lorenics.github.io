Refunds and cancellation

1. Cancellation

You can cancel your membership at any time. Open the receipt email you got when you joined, click "Manage membership" or "Subscription settings", then click "Cancel membership" on the next screen.

If you cancel before your next payment date, you will not be charged for the next period (the next month on the monthly plan, the next year on the yearly plan).

After you cancel, you keep access to the member problems until the end of the period you have already paid for (one month, or one year).

2. Refunds

The membership is a digital service. Every problem is available right after payment. For this reason, we do not refund payments as a rule, and we do not give partial refunds for the rest of a period after you cancel. This also applies to the yearly plan.

Before you join, please use the free part (walk the factory, run the machine, three problems) to check that FA World runs on your computer.

3. If a fault on our side kept you out

If you could not use the member problems because of a fault on our side, email us. We will refund you for the time you could not use them.

4. Double charges and charges you do not recognize

If you were charged twice for the same period, or see a charge you do not recognize, email us. We will check and refund it.

5. Your legal rights

If the law where you live gives you a right to cancel or to a refund, this policy does not limit that right.

FA World Privacy

This explains the information Lorenics Inc. ("we") handles in FA World. For anything not covered here, our Privacy Policy applies (https://lorenics.com/privacy.html, in Japanese).

1. When you play for free

We do not ask for your name. There is no sign-up. You give an email address only if you want news about new problems (see 3).

We use no advertising tools and no third-party analytics tools.

We may count how many times FA World is used. We count only five things: the walk was started, the first free problem was finished, all three free problems were finished, the membership prompt was shown, and a join button was pressed. We do not attach or store anything that shows who you are with these counts (no name, email address, IP address, cookie or license key).

2. What stays in your browser

We save the following in your browser. It is not sent to us.

  • Your language choice
  • Your progress and results
  • Your practice log (including the name you typed for the log)
  • The ladder programs you made
  • Your license key

You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

3. When you join

You join and pay on Gumroad (a company in the United States). Gumroad handles your card details. We do not receive them.

We receive from Gumroad information such as your name, your email address, your payment status and your country.

The membership prompt may have an email field for news about new problems. It is optional. The address you enter is sent to Gumroad's subscribe feature, and we use it only to send you that news. You can stop at any time with the link in each email.

4. Checking your license key

When you enter your license key, and the next time you open FA World, your browser sends the key to Gumroad to check it.

5. What we use it for

  • Checking that you are a member
  • Answering and contacting you
  • Telling you about changes to the price or the Service
  • News about new problems (only if you gave your email address)
  • Knowing how many times the Service is used (counts only)
  • Preventing improper use

6. Contact

If you email us, we use your email address and message to answer you.

7. Hosting

FA World is hosted on an outside service. The hosting provider may keep connection records (IP address, date and time, and so on).

8. Requests and contact

You can ask us to disclose, correct or delete your personal information, as set out in our Privacy Policy.
